ShopTech Return and Refund Policy (fictional sample document)

Returns within 30 days
You can return most products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they are
in their original condition and packaging.

Exceptions
- Opened headphones and earbuds can only be returned within 14 days, for hygiene reasons.
- Laptops that were opened and set up are subject to a 10% restocking fee.
- Gift cards and downloadable software cannot be returned.

Refunds
Refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item
has been inspected. You will receive an email when the refund is issued.

Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, contact us within 48 hours.
We cover the return shipping and send a replacement immediately.
