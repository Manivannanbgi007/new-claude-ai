# ShopTech Warranty Policy (fictional sample document)

## Standard warranty
All laptops, tablets and smartphones sold by ShopTech come with a **2-year limited warranty**
from the date of delivery. Headphones and monitors have a **1-year limited warranty**.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects such as a failing battery (below 80% capacity within
the first year), dead pixels (more than three on a single display), defective keyboards and
faulty charging ports.

The warranty does NOT cover accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic scratches, or damage
caused by unauthorised repairs.

## CarePlus extended protection
CarePlus extends the warranty by 2 additional years and adds accidental damage cover
(one incident per year). CarePlus costs $149 for laptops and $79 for smartphones and tablets.
It must be purchased within 30 days of delivery.

## How to make a warranty claim
1. Open a support ticket at support.shoptech.example with your order id.
2. Describe the problem and attach a photo or video if possible.
3. We send a prepaid shipping label within 2 business days.
4. Repairs are completed within 10 business days after we receive the device.
